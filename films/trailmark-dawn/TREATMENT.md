# TREATMENT — Trailmark · «بلا شبكة» (8 s vertical test)

Style: `silhouette-rhythm`. Purpose of this film: **a test**, not a launch film — to
judge whether this library's motion and rendering clear the bar the previous Remotion
ads failed (measured: 3.6–4.8 s of dead frames per film).

Product: Trailmark — offline route tracking for walk, run, cycling, hiking.
No account, no cloud. Based on the app's own README.

---

## 1. Three candidate structures

**A. One runner, one line.** A lone runner crosses the ridge in profile at dawn; the
route line is drawn behind them on the same beat as the footfalls. Signal bars in the
corner fall to nothing; the line keeps going. Ends on the whole track drawn across an
empty map. *Shape: a line that survives losing the world.*

**B. The countdown of a run.** Four beats of the same figure at four moments, each one
faster, the sun sinking; each section a different framing. *Shape: time passing.*

**C. Two runners, one uphill.** A call-and-response between a runner and a cyclist on
opposite sides of the water, on alternating beats. *Shape: duet.*

**Chosen: A.** It is the product's one true idea (works with no network) and it is
expressible in eight seconds without a word. B spends its time on time, not on the
claim; C needs two casts and reads as a crowd, not a product.

## 2. Logline and arc

*Logline:* the signal dies, the line doesn't.
Setup: a runner alone on a ridge, sun low, everything moving on the beat.
Turn: the signal indicator empties — and the route line keeps drawing.
Ending: the full route on an empty map, one line of type.

## 3. Benchmark

- **Learn:** beat-locked silhouette advertising — pose changes land on the beat and hold;
  a long telephoto that makes the sun the stage; the black wipe as a cut.
- **Don't take:** any existing campaign's figures, layouts, music or typeface.

## 4. Shot list

| # | Shot | Framing | Angle | Camera | Dur | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ridge, runner enters from right | wide, sun half frame | low, at horizon | slow lateral track, right→left | 2.2 s | establishes the light and the loneliness before the claim |
| 2 | Runner in profile, stride on the beat | medium, subject ≥ 1/3 height | level, sun behind | locked, feet planted | 2.4 s | the beat itself; the body reads at thumbnail size |
| 3 | Signal dies, line keeps drawing | medium, line leads into frame | level | push in | 2.0 s | the turn: the only thing that changes is the network |
| 4 | Whole route on empty map + line | high wide | top-down-ish | slow pull back | 1.4 s | scale reveal; the ending echoes shot 1's loneliness inverted |

Signature shot: **shot 3** — a black wipe where the runner passes the lens and the line
is already on the other side of the cut.

## 5. Beat sheet (96 bpm, 8.0 s = 12.8 beats → 3 bars of 4 at 90 bpm)

90 bpm → beat = 0.667 s; 8 s = 12 beats.

| Beat | t | Picture | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.00 | ridge, sun, first footfall | wind bed, low log drum on the footfall |
| 2 | 0.67 | runner stride 2 | woodblock, water drip |
| 3 | 1.33 | stride 3, route line starts | marimba note joins |
| 4 | 2.00 | cut: profile medium | full kit enters |
| 5 | 2.67 | stride, line passes the lens | clap |
| 6 | 3.33 | stride | shaker double-time |
| 7 | 4.00 | **signal bars empty — music stops** | near-silence: wind only |
| 8 | 4.67 | line keeps drawing | single marimba, alone |
| 9 | 5.33 | push in, sun touches horizon | held string chord |
| 10 | 6.00 | black wipe | whoosh |
| 11 | 6.67 | map, whole route | chord resolves |
| 12 | 7.33 | type lands | tail |

## 6. Sound design

Ambience: wind over open ground, thinning at the turn. Foley: footfalls (pan with the
figure), one water drip per beat in shot 1, one whoosh for the wipe. Music: marimba,
log drum, dry kit, shaker; strings only under the held chord. Two silences: beat 7
(music cut dead) and the half-beat before the type.

## 7. Type

One line only, in Arabic, cream on the dark lower water, left-aligned in the lower
third — the style's rule. Face: an OFL Arabic sans of the same weight class as the
style's condensed sans (Cairo or IBM Plex Sans Arabic), self-hosted, subset.

Line: **«بلا شبكة. المسار يكمل.»**

## 8. What this film deliberately does not do

No UI screenshots, no logo animation, no voice, no subtitles — the point is to test the
style's motion and rendering, not the campaign.
